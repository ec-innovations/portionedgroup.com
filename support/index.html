Common questions

When can I download Portioned?

Portioned is in its final round of testing. It will be on the App Store and Google Play soon. Email us if you’d like to hear when it’s out.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later, and Android phones with Android 8.0 or later.

Where is my information kept?

On your phone. Information leaves it only when you use a feature that needs our server, such as an AI feature or a brand food search. Our Privacy Policy will explain the details.

How do plans and cancelling work?

Portioned is free to start. Paid plans are bought through the App Store or Google Play, and you can cancel any time in your store account’s subscription settings. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel a plan.

Is Portioned medical advice?

No. Portioned gives general information for everyday eating. Talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before changing how you eat, especially if you have a medical condition or take medication.
